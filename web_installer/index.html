👉 INSTALL YANIS VISION THROUGH THE WALL ON YOUR ESP32

No ESP-IDF, VS Code, Python, or compilation required. Just connect your ESP32 to your computer with USB and install Yanis Vision directly from your browser.

⚠️ Web Serial is not available in this browser. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop computer (over HTTPS or localhost).
Use Chrome or Edge on desktop to install. This page must be served over HTTPS or localhost.

Plug in your ESP32 with a data-capable USB cable, click the button, pick the serial port and confirm. If the port does not show up, hold the BOOT button while connecting.

After installation

  1. The ESP32 creates a Wi-Fi network called: YanisVision-Setup
  2. Connect using your phone or computer.
  3. The setup portal opens automatically (Captive Portal). If it doesn't, browse to http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Enter your Wi-Fi credentials.
  5. Access the live dashboard at: http://yanisvision.local
